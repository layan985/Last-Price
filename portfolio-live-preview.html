03 / RESEARCH FAILURE LOG

Three times the preferred story lost.

Good research objects should preserve the diagnostics that killed attractive claims.

FAILURE 01 · FORECASTING

The index did not earn predictive status.

Original idea: JCCI-Observable might improve rolling-origin prediction and reveal a robust state-dependent conversion mechanism.

Diagnostic: adding it worsened forecast accuracy; exploratory local projections did not establish robust state dependence.

Change: retained it only as descriptive diagnostics and reframed the paper around an employment–productivity conversion wedge.

FAILURE 02 · CAUSAL CLAIM

The deadline effect did not survive falsification.

Original idea: Germany’s PFOF exemption expiry caused a discontinuous venue-share migration.

Diagnostic: placebo timing, pre-trend and exact-rank checks weakened the causal interpretation; trend-adjusted discontinuity was about 0.16 with SE 0.54, exact p .40.

Change: concluded broader-period reallocation, not a credible causal deadline effect.

FAILURE 03 · THEORY

A first-order condition was not enough.

Original idea: a smooth logit speed contest delivered the claimed symmetric equilibrium.

Diagnostic: the contest can become locally convex at high speed sensitivity, so the symmetric FOC solution need not be a true equilibrium.

Change: rebuilt the baseline with a bounded affine priority lottery on a compact interval, restoring global strict concavity and a genuine unique optimum.
